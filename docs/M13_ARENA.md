# M13 arena blockout

Visual pass: `ArenaVisualUpgrade` incrementally upgrades both new and already completed blockouts without deleting the world. It adds mirrored spruce groves, sparse LOS-blocking rock walls, passable fern/grass bush areas, camp ground rings, colored tower crowns and throne plinths, and defined base borders. The layout reserves wide clearance around lane paths, spawns, towers, thrones, camps and bush entrances. Its hidden completion marker is under the floor. The pass writes only generated floor materials and empty space (or the old single-block oak-leaf bush marker); unknown player-built blocks are left alone. Vanilla mobs are rejected on arena entity load; Zeravorn actor entities are preserved.

Recovery note: the first blockout version could fail after decorating because MID crossed the red throne pillar. MID now detours around both friendly throne pillars, and config validation rejects path waypoints inside throne or bush blocks before construction. On entry, an unmarked world matching the generated perimeter, all throne/tower pillars, camps and bushes is revalidated and marked complete without clearing or rebuilding it. Any other partial or modified build remains untouched and is rejected for manual inspection.

The source of truth is `src/main/resources/config_defaults/arena_v1.json`. `MapLoader` reads it and `MapValidator` rejects missing bases, paths, towers, camps, bush IDs, unsafe spawn/fountain placement, duplicate IDs, out-of-bounds zones, invalid tower order, path gaps above 12 blocks and paths through tower hitboxes. JSON lane points are turn/control points; the loader interpolates runtime waypoints at most 12 blocks apart. The current flat blockout uses its configured spawn Y, not an assumed universal Y=64.

The playable dimension is `zeravorn:arena_v1` (`src/main/resources/data/zeravorn/dimension/arena_v1.json`). On first entry, `ArenaWorld` scans its reserved 221×221 build area before writing anything; occupied space is rejected rather than overwritten. It then builds the floor/walls in bounded batches, marks the three routes, bases, tower/throne pillars, camp positions and bush centers, checks the built paths and spawn clearance, and writes a lodestone completion marker last. Existing completed worlds are reused. A partially interrupted build is intentionally not auto-overwritten; inspect the dimension manually before recovery.

Test: enter the lobby, select team/spell/hero and Ready. LOADING lasts while the new dimension is generated; after transfer and countdown you should stand on the blue/red base of the 220×220 blockout. Walk each lane. F9 toggles a logical map overlay with boundary, lane points, tower markers/radii, camp leash, bush positions, fountain danger areas and the player's vision radius. The prior `zeravorn:test_arena` dimension remains untouched but is no longer the default match destination.

Runtime actor pass: `ArenaActorRuntime` creates blue and red waves on all three configured paths at 00:30 and every 30 seconds thereafter. Each wave has three warriors and two ranged creeps, plus a siege creep every third wave. Creeps march between waypoints and attack enemy creeps they meet. Jungle camps appear at 00:35 and respawn on the configured mob timer after a kill. The client renders the authored cuboid textures and samples Blockbench bone animation tracks for lane and jungle actors. The MOBA basic-attack request resolves arena actors from server aim and range, so left-clicking an enemy creep or camp deals damage; camp kills use the existing gold, experience and buff service. Amelia's authored rigid mesh and animation clips replace the local player's third-person vanilla avatar when selected. Her gameplay abilities remain connected through `ConfiguredArenaEntry`; open C to spend a skill point before casting Q/E/R.

Current gameplay boundary: player-vs-player basic attacks, abilities and Flash use live player positions, block LOS and safe-endpoint checks through `ArenaWorldQueries` and existing server combat services. Amelia Q/E/R also damage lane and jungle actors after their normal server-side cast validation. Jungle actors chase nearby heroes, attack and leash back to camp. Lane actors now spawn in two staggered columns and stop before overlapping another living actor. Jungle actors expose their HP above the model. Hero death freezes movement, switches the local camera to third person, and starts the level-based respawn timer; at expiry the server returns the hero to the team spawn with full HP and Mana. The HUD shows authoritative ability cooldowns. Retribution and other heroes' abilities still use hero-only world queries; structure combat and Recall are not yet connected. Verify the custom renderers inside a launched match before treating the runtime visuals as accepted.

The arena dimension has its own paused noon clock and weather is kept clear. Vanilla damage cannot kill a player in the arena; the Minecraft health and hunger values are kept full, while `HeroRuntime` applies the configured HP/Mana regeneration and remains the only gameplay death/respawn authority. The client hides survival hearts, hunger, experience, hotbar and item tooltip while in the arena, and the gameplay HUD no longer reserves a line for inventory text.

For a hand-built replacement, preserve the `zeravorn:arena_v1` dimension ID and align the JSON coordinates with the actual world. The conservative builder will refuse to overwrite an occupied reserved area. A completed custom world needs a deliberate completion-marker/integration path; do not place the marker to bypass validation without checking collision, paths and structures against the config.
